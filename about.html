The Inspiring Biography

From the Footpaths of Chandigarh to Global World Records.

Janvi Jindal is India's highest Guinness World Record-holding female athlete, and the second-highest individual sportsperson in India after Sachin Tendulkar.

Chapter 01 • Genesis

Starting at Age 9 on Concrete Sidewalks

Unlike international athletes who train in multimillion-dollar temperature-controlled sports complexes, Janvi’s skating journey began on the public footpaths and parking lots outside the showrooms of Sector 22, Chandigarh.

Fascinated by the fluid momentum of inline wheels, she did not have access to an elite personal coaching team. Instead, she and her father, Munish Jindal, took up the challenge together.

Chapter 02 • The Mentorship

YouTube Tutorials & Evening Practice Sessions

After working hours, her father spent nights dissecting high-frame-rate videos of European and Asian freestyle slalom world champions on YouTube. They studied wheel angles, foot pivots, edge pressures, and rotational velocity.

"We would mark chalk lines on the road outside our house, set up plastic cones, and repeat the same spin or cross-slalom run dozens of times until muscle memory became automatic."
Chapter 03 • Global Recognition

11 Guinness Records & 2× State Awardee

Janvi shattered record after record: high-speed 360-degree inline spins in 30 seconds, two-wheel slalom agility, continuous wheelings, and a daring stunt spinning at 17,982 ft at Khardung La Pass in Ladakh.

She became the youngest person in Chandigarh’s history to be conferred both the State Award and the Municipal Corporation Award twice (Arts & Culture in 2021, and Sports in 2025).

Chapter 04 • Holistic Excellence

Sports, Studies & Culture: A Girl Who Merged All Together

Disproving the myth that sports hinders academic performance, Janvi achieved top academic honors in her CBSE examinations, proving that discipline on wheels translates directly to discipline in the classroom.
