Federation Standards

Official Freestyle Slalom Competition Regulations

All official competitions sanctioned by the Roller Skating Federation of India (RSFI) and World Skate follow standard technical parameters:

📏 Cone Spacing Lines

Standard competition courses consist of three parallel lines:

  • • 50 cm Line: 20 cones (Technical fast feet)
  • • 80 cm Line: 20 cones (Classic freestyle rhythm)
  • • 120 cm Line: 14 cones (Power sit-tricks & wheelings)
⏱️ Timing & Music

Classic Slalom routines run strictly between 105 to 120 seconds (1 min 45s to 2 mins) accompanied by music.

  • • Performance starts on first motion
  • • Over/under time incurs penalty deductions
  • • Artistic rhythm score depends on dhol/beat sync
⚠️ Penalties & Deductions

Strict deductions apply during competition rounds:

  • • Touching a cone: 0.5 point deduction
  • • Kicking a cone out: 1.0 point deduction
  • • Major fall: 5.0 points deduction

⛸️ Janvi's Gear Advice for Indian Concrete & Asphalt Roads

Hard Boot Shells Always

Avoid soft recreational fabric shoes. Hard composite or plastic shells provide mandatory ankle support to prevent rolling and sprains on uneven Indian footpaths.

Wheel Hardness: 84A to 86A

For rough outdoor roads, wheels rated 84A or 85A balance grip with high durability, preventing premature chipping on hot pavement.

Rockered Slalom Frame

For freestyle slalom, mount 76mm wheels on the ends and 80mm wheels in the center (banana rocker) to replicate ice blade curve dynamics.
