The Footpath Chronicles

Trained by a Father. Inspired by YouTube. Ready for the World.

Without expensive international sports arenas or high-priced foreign coaches, Janvi and her father Munish Jindal analyzed world championship clips online after working hours, carving out slalom lines on public roads and showroom footpaths in Sector 22, Chandigarh.

State & National Honours

Youngest 2× State Awardee in Chandigarh History

Janvi is the only public figure from Chandigarh to be honored twice with both the State Award and the Municipal Corporation Award (in Arts & Culture in 2021 at age 12, and in Sports in 2025). Felicitated by Member of Parliament and Chandigarh University Chancellor Satnam Singh Sandhu with prestigious academic honors.
